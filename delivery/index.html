Execution & Delivery Playbook

Some days you're a people manager, some days an incident leader. Every day, you also wear a TPM hat: making sure the team's planned work flows, finishes, and lands on the things that matter. This playbook covers running the SRE daily over your Linear board, keeping Linear honest, planning capacity, and managing dependencies.

  • The TPM hat owns flow, priority, dependencies, and predictability. It doesn't own the technical solution.
  • Walk the board right to left in the daily. Finishing beats starting.
  • Use PULSE on any in-flight item: Priority, Unblock, Load, Scope, ETA.
  • Plan to 80%, not 100%. SRE teams always get interrupted.

Say Which Hat You're Wearing

"With my TPM hat on: can we get this out of review today?" lands differently from the same words from "the boss". Naming the hat keeps the daily about flow, not performance.

The Plays

1. Run the SRE Daily

Fifteen minutes in the US/India overlap. Walk the board right to left, PULSE the stuck items, and send problem-solving to the parking lot.

2. Keep Linear Honest

States with clear entry and exit rules, a label taxonomy, priorities that mean something, and saved views for you.

3. Plan Capacity

Cycle planning with a split for projects, toil, and interrupts, prioritization tied to error budgets, and flow metrics.

4. Manage Dependencies & Risk

A RAID log, dependency requests to CFTs and Cloud Infrastructure, and when to escalate.

Frameworks Used

  • PULSE: Priority, Unblock, Load, Scope, ETA, for any item in flight.
  • Walk the Board: right to left, Done → In Review → In Progress → Todo.
  • Definition of Ready / Done: the entry and exit rules for work.
  • Capacity Split: projects, KTLO and toil, interrupt buffer.
  • RAID: Risks, Assumptions, Issues, Dependencies.

Templates

Standup agenda · Linear issue template · Cycle planning · Project update · RAID log · Dependency request

Weak / Good / Best

Weak

The daily is a round-robin status report to the manager. Linear is out of date, so nobody trusts it. Blockers get mentioned for days without action.

Good

The board is current before the call. The daily walks the board, blockers get an owner and a date, and the cycle plan leaves room for interrupts.

Best

Facilitation rotates across regions, flow metrics guide planning, dependencies are escalated before they bite, and the Ops lead can run it all without you.
