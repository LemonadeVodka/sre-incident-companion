The LAYERS Framework

Six questions, asked in order and at the right layer, turn a vague "the AI is slow" into a located, measured, evidenced problem with an owner and a plan. Each question comes in three versions: one that helps the NOC see, one that makes SRE engineers think, and one that tells executives what matters.

  • Locate · Assess · Yesterday · Evidence · Response · Sustain.
  • In a live incident use L-A-Y-E-R. In weekly reviews use A-Y-E-S. In design reviews use L-R-S. In postmortems use Y-E-R-S.
  • Your goal isn't to know the answers. It's to make sure someone can answer with data, and to notice when they can't.

L · Locate: Which Layer Is It In?

Where in the path is this happening: device, edge, gateway, orchestrator, feature, partner, or model?

Ask the NOC (plain English)
  • Which feature is affected? One, or many?
  • Which sweep query shows it? Send me the screenshot.
  • Is it one client version, one region, or one partner, or everyone?
Ask SRE (spur technical thinking)
  • Which layer is returning the errors or adding the time? How do you know it's that layer and not the one in front of it?
  • Does the layer comparison (edge vs gateway vs service vs model) agree with your theory?
  • If this layer were healthy, what would we expect to see one layer up and one layer down?
Tell executives
  • The problem is in [layer], which affects [features]. The rest of the product is working normally.
A Good Answer

Names a layer and shows the data that places it there, for example: "Errors start at the gateway. Edge traffic is normal, and backends barely see the requests."

Red Flags
  • "The AI is slow." (no layer)
  • "It's probably the network." (no evidence)
  • Different people name different layers and nobody reconciles them

A · Assess: How Bad, and for Whom?

How many users are affected, how far from normal, and is it the typical experience (P50) or the bad-day experience (P95)?

Ask the NOC (plain English)
  • What is the number now, and what is normal for this hour?
  • Is it more than 2× normal errors, or 1.5× normal P95?
  • Is it getting better, worse, or flat over the last 15 minutes?
Ask SRE (spur technical thinking)
  • Is P50 moving or only P95? What does that tell you about who is affected?
  • What share of requests or users is affected? Are we counting with itemCount (sampling)?
  • Is the error rate counting the right things: user-visible failures, not retries we recover from?
Tell executives
  • About [X%] of [feature] users are seeing [slow responses / errors] for [duration]. Normal is [baseline].
A Good Answer

A number, a baseline, and a trend: "P95 is 14 s against a normal 6 s, about 1 in 8 Writing Assist users, rising for 20 minutes."

Red Flags
  • "Lots of errors."
  • An average instead of P50/P95
  • No baseline, so nobody knows whether this is normal

Y · Yesterday: Compared to What? What Changed?

What is normal for this time, and what changed just before it started?

Ask the NOC (plain English)
  • Did this happen at the same time yesterday or last week?
  • Was there a release, rollout, or partner notice in the last 24 hours?
  • When exactly did it start? Write down the time.
Ask SRE (spur technical thinking)
  • What's the first timestamp it deviates from baseline, and what changed within an hour before it (deploys, flags, model version, routing policy, partner changes)?
  • Could traffic itself have changed (a launch, a time zone waking up, a batch job)?
  • If nothing changed on our side, what changed on someone else's (Cloud Infrastructure, partner, provider)?
Tell executives
  • It started at [time] and lines up with [change / no known change yet].
A Good Answer

A start time and a candidate change, with a correlation shown on a chart.

Red Flags
  • "Nothing changed." (without checking the deploy and change logs)
  • Comparing with the wrong baseline (a weekday vs a weekend)

E · Evidence: How Sure Are We?

What data shows that, how confident are you, and what would prove us wrong?

Ask the NOC (plain English)
  • Which query or screen shows this? Paste the link.
  • Did two different checks show the same thing?
Ask SRE (spur technical thinking)
  • What's your confidence: low, medium, or high? What would raise it?
  • What else could explain the same chart? How would we rule it out cheaply?
  • Is any telemetry missing or sampled that could be hiding the real picture?
Tell executives
  • We're [highly confident / fairly confident / still investigating] that the cause is [X].
A Good Answer

States confidence honestly and names the next check that would confirm it or rule it out.

Red Flags
  • Certainty without data
  • A single hypothesis defended against all evidence
  • "We can't see that." accepted without a plan to get visibility

R · Response: What Are We Doing About It?

What's the safest reversible fix, who owns it, what does it cost, and when will we know it worked?

Ask the NOC (plain English)
  • Who owns this layer, and have they been paged? When?
  • Which pre-agreed request fits (pause, halt, restart, banner)? Has it been sent?
  • When is the next update?
Ask SRE (spur technical thinking)
  • What's the most reversible mitigation, and what's the risk if it's wrong?
  • Can we degrade gracefully instead of failing (a smaller model, no retrieval, disable one partner connector)?
  • What decision do you need from me, and by when?
Tell executives
  • We're [action] now. We expect improvement by [time]. [Decision needed: X / No decision needed.]
A Good Answer

One owner, one reversible action, an expected effect, and a check time. Decisions are escalated with options and cost.

Red Flags
  • Debugging the root cause while users stay broken
  • "Someone should…" (no owner)
  • A risky change with no undo plan

S · Sustain: How Do We Keep It From Coming Back?

What will we change so we see this sooner next time, or never see it again?

Ask the NOC (plain English)
  • What would have helped you spot this earlier?
  • Which runbook or sweep query should we add or fix?
Ask SRE (spur technical thinking)
  • Is this a detection gap, a design gap, or a process gap? What's the one change with the most leverage?
  • Should this become an SLO, an alert on burn rate, a release gate, or an architectural change (isolation, circuit breaker, capacity)?
  • Which postmortem actions do you need me to get funded or prioritized?
Tell executives
  • To prevent recurrence we're [actions] by [dates]. We need [investment / nothing].
A Good Answer

2–5 specific, owned, dated actions that change the system, not "be more careful".

Red Flags
  • No postmortem
  • Actions with no owner or date
  • Repeating last quarter's action items

Device & On-Device Model
Client team (Nimbus: Client CFT)		 Latency: Quick tasks slow on some devices. Slow app startup. The local model is slow on older hardware or a new driver.
Errors: Crashes, local model failures, TLS/proxy failures that never reach the server, clock skew causing 401s.
  • Is it one device model, one client version, or one driver version?
  • Do support tickets mention a specific laptop or update?
  • Is the crash or error tied to the newest client version?
  • Is the server quiet even though users complain? (The failure may be before the cloud.)
  • Is client telemetry showing local inference time by hardware or driver?
  • Did a local model or driver update ship? What's its rollout percentage?
  • Are failures visible only in client telemetry? What's our coverage there?
  • Should we halt the client or model rollout? What's the health gate?
 Some users on [device/version] see slower quick tasks. Cloud features are unaffected.

A recent app update is failing for [X%] of users. The rollout is paused.
Device Orchestrator (Local vs Cloud)
Client team (Nimbus: Client CFT)		 Latency: Cloud traffic and latency rise with no server change, because local answers are falling back to the cloud.
Errors: Routing misconfiguration sends requests to the wrong place. A fallback surge triggers cloud 429s.
  • Did the share of local answers drop (sweep 7)?
  • Is a client or local model rollout in progress right now?
  • Did 429s start at the same time as a client rollout?
  • What's the fallback rate by local model version? Does the rise follow the rollout curve?
  • Is the cloud sized for a fallback surge? Do we cap fallback traffic per version?
  • Was the routing policy changed? Is it canaried?
  • Is fallback rate part of the release gate?
 A device update is pushing extra work to the cloud, slowing [features]. We've paused the update and added capacity.

A routing change caused errors for [feature]. It has been reverted.
Sync & Relay (Cross-Device)
Client team + platform team (Nimbus: Client CFT, hosted by Cloud Infrastructure)		 Latency: Cross-device features (Next Step, cross-device Catch Up) lag. Context shows up late on the second device.
Errors: Pairing or key-exchange failures, relay 5xx, "can't connect your devices".
  • Is it only cross-device features? Does single-device use work?
  • Is relay latency or error rate up?
  • Are sync errors tied to one client version or OS?
  • Is the relay saturated, or is pairing failing for one client version or OS?
  • Did enterprise firewalls push more traffic from peer-to-peer onto the relay?
  • Relay-side failure or a client pairing bug? Content is end-to-end encrypted, so what metadata do we have?
 Moving between devices is slower than normal. Each device on its own works fine.

Some users can't connect their devices. Using one device still works. A fix is in progress.
Edge / CDN / WAF
Platform team (Nimbus: Cloud Infrastructure)		 Latency: Slow everywhere at once. Origin timeouts (504).
Errors: 502/503/504 at the edge, 403 from WAF rules. Our services may look quiet.
  • Is the edge 5xx rate up (sweep 3)?
  • Is it all regions or one?
  • Is App Insights unusually quiet while users can't connect?
  • Are there 403s? Did a security rule change?
  • Is the edge slow, or is it waiting on the origin behind it? Compare edge time with gateway time.
  • Any CDN, DNS, or certificate change?
  • What does the edge error info say (origin connection, timeout, probe)?
  • Can we roll back the WAF/CDN change safely? Who approves?
 Our internet entry point is slow for [regions]. The platform team is on it with the provider.

Many users can't connect because of a problem at our network entry point. We're rolling back a recent change.
Gateway & Auth
Platform team (Nimbus: Cloud Infrastructure)		 Latency: Added time in the gateway itself (policies, token validation).
Errors: 401s (token rejected), 429s (rate limits), auth config changes, signing key rollover.
  • Is it slow for all features?
  • Are 401s on all APIs or one (sweep 4)? One client version or all?
  • What's gateway time minus backend time? Did a policy change add work per request?
  • Which layer rejected the token? Cohort: everyone, one build, or scattered devices (clock skew)?
  • Did any secret, certificate, or policy change or expire?
 A gateway configuration is adding delay to all features.

Some users are being asked to sign in again or are blocked. We've found the configuration change and are reverting it.
Cloud Orchestrator
Platform team (Nimbus: Cloud Infrastructure)		 Latency: Every feature slows together while models and services look normal inside.
Errors: Errors across all features at once.
  • Are all features slow at the same time?
  • Did errors start in all features at the same minute?
  • Is the orchestrator saturated, or is one feature exhausting it? Do we have per-feature bulkheads?
  • Was a routing change deployed? Is there a canary for routing changes?
 A shared routing component is slowing every feature. The platform team is scaling it.

A shared component failed and affected every feature. We've rolled it back.
Feature Services & Retrieval
Feature teams (Nimbus: feature CFTs)		 Latency: One feature slow. Retrieval slow. Bigger prompts. A stalled instance (Rust runtime).
Errors: 5xx or panics in one feature, failing dependencies.
  • Is it only one feature? Which one?
  • Is one instance much slower than others?
  • Did errors start after this team's deploy?
  • Where is the time: our code, retrieval, or the model call? Show me P50/P95 per hop.
  • Did prompts or context grow (token growth)? Did a deploy add work to the hot path?
  • Top exceptions and panics? Correlated with application_Version?
  • Can we degrade gracefully (answer without retrieval)?
 [Feature] is slower after a recent change. The team is rolling it back.

[Feature] is failing for [X%] of requests after a change. We're rolling it back.
Integration Platform & Partners
Integrations team (Nimbus: Partner Integrations CFT) + the iPaaS vendor		 Latency: One feature slow only for users of one partner. Partner API slow. Webhooks delayed.
Errors: Partner 5xx, partner rate limits (429), expired OAuth tokens or consent (401), API/schema changes.
  • Is it one partner, or everything that goes through the integration platform?
  • What do the partner's and the iPaaS vendor's status pages say?
  • Are errors only for one partner's users?
  • Has the partner escalation contact been notified?
  • Are our timeouts to the partner shorter than our own latency budget?
  • Do we have a circuit breaker and a degraded mode per partner?
  • Is it partner-side (status page, their errors) or our connector (schema change, token refresh)?
  • Can we disable just this connector with a flag and keep the rest working?
 A third-party partner ([partner]) is slow, affecting [feature] for their users. We've switched to a fallback and contacted them.

[Partner] is having an outage. Users of [feature] with [partner] are affected. Nothing is wrong with our systems. We've disabled the integration temporarily and are working with the partner.
Model Provider (Capacity & Quota)
Platform team (Nimbus: Cloud Infrastructure, with Azure)		 Latency: Slow first token (queueing), longer outputs, region problems, capacity saturation.
Errors: 429 throttling (noisy neighbor, retry storms), provider 5xx, content-filter blocks.
  • Are 429s or model latency up on one deployment or all?
  • Is it 429 or 5xx? (Different owners and fixes.)
  • Is Azure status green?
  • First-token time up or generation time up? Which tells us capacity vs output length?
  • How close are we to quota at peak? Who is using it (tokens per minute by caller)?
  • Did the model version change (auto-upgrade)?
  • Is a background workload or retry storm consuming quota?
  • Should we fail over regions? What's the capacity and data-residency impact?
 Our AI provider capacity is saturated, so responses are slower. We're adding overflow capacity at about $[X] per hour.

Our AI provider is [throttling / failing]. We're [shifting load / failing over]. The provider has been engaged.
Telemetry & Visibility
Platform team (Nimbus: Cloud Infrastructure Observability)		 Latency: Charts lag or show gaps. Sampling hides the slow tail.
Errors: Users complain while dashboards stay green. Traces break between layers.
  • Did data volume drop (sweep 6)? Missing data looks like health.
  • Users complain, but our numbers look fine? Raise it anyway.
  • Is the collector healthy? Is sampling hiding the P95 tail?
  • Which part of the path is invisible to us? What would it take to see it?
 We have a gap in monitoring that delayed detection. Closing it is an action item.

Customers noticed before our monitoring did. We're fixing that blind spot.

Using It Well

Ask, Then Listen

One question at a time. Let silence work. Junior engineers often need a few seconds before they answer.

Curious, Not Interrogating

"Help me understand…" lands better than "Why didn't you…". The goal is shared understanding.

Ask Once, in the Channel

During incidents, ask in the incident channel through the coordinator, not in DMs.

Notice Missing Answers

"We can't see that" is a finding. Write it down as a Sustain action.
