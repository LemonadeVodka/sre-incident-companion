Be the Expert in the Room

You don't need to write the queries. You need to ask the questions that make your NOC see clearly, make your SRE engineers think harder, and let you give your executives a clear, honest picture. This track gives you a framework to do that for an AI product, layer by layer, using the two signals that matter most: latency and error rates.

  • LAYERS is a six-step question loop: Locate, Assess, Yesterday, Evidence, Response, Sustain.
  • Ask it at the right layer: device, local/cloud routing, edge, gateway, cloud orchestrator, feature services, partner integrations, model provider, or telemetry.
  • Every step has a version for the NOC (simple, scripted), for SRE engineers (spurs technical thinking), and for executives (business translation).
  • Use it in four places: live incidents, weekly reviews, design reviews, and postmortems.

The LAYERS Framework

LLocateWhich layer is it in? AAssessHow bad, and for whom? YYesterdayCompared to what? What changed? EEvidenceHow sure are we? RResponseWhat are we doing about it? SSustainHow do we stop it coming back?

Your Four Jobs

1. Ask the Right Questions

LAYERS, at the right layer, for latency and errors. Make the team think, without doing their work for them.

2. Enable the NOC

An offshore team with growing technical skills: plain English, scripts, a competency ladder, and safety to escalate.

3. Develop SRE Engineers

Question ladders from junior to senior that bring out the technical considerations.

4. Update Executives

BLUF and SITREP: impact in business terms, honest confidence, and the decisions you need.

Learning Path

  1. Know the LayersA mental model of an AI product, from the device to the model provider and partners. About 15 minutes.
  2. Latency & Errors PrimerP50/P95, time to first token, and what each error code means and which layer sends it. About 20 minutes.
  3. The LAYERS FrameworkThe six steps, good vs red-flag answers, and the Question Builder. About 30 minutes, then practice.
  4. Enabling the NOC and Enabling SRE EngineersCoaching through questions.
  5. Updating ExecutivesThe SITREP builder and translation table.
  6. During an Incident, then Post-Incident ActionsLAYERS in live mode, then the After Action Review.
  7. Operating RhythmThe weekly latency and error review, monthly health check, and quarterly exec report.
