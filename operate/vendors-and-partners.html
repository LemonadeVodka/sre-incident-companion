Vendors & Partners

A large share of your reliability depends on companies you don't control: the NOC vendor, the integration platform, the cloud and model provider, and partners like meeting platforms and OEM device APIs. Managing them well means clear commitments, known escalation paths, and regular, evidence-based reviews.

  • For every third party: owner, contract and SLA, escalation path, status page, and review cadence. Write them down.
  • Contain, then work the relationship: in incidents, protect users first (kill switch, fallback), then escalate to the partner.
  • Review each one quarterly with data: incidents caused, SLA attainment, open asks.

Third Parties in Incidents

  1. Confirm it's them.Their status page, our partner and iPaaS error breakdown (query), and whether only their users are affected.
  2. Contain.A per-partner kill switch or fallback, so the rest of the product stays healthy.
  3. Escalate through the right path, with evidence: timestamps, error rates, request IDs.
  4. Communicate.The SITREP names the partner factually ("our systems are fine"), and you agree public wording with the partner team.
  5. Follow up.Request their incident report, record the SLA impact, and raise it at the quarterly review.

Quarterly Partner Review

  • Availability and latency for each partner over the quarter (from our telemetry, not just theirs).
  • Incidents caused, time to their acknowledgement, and quality of their root-cause reports.
  • SLA breaches and credits claimed.
  • Upcoming changes or deprecations on their side, and whether we're ready for them.
  • Our resilience: does a circuit breaker and degraded mode exist for this partner?
