The Senior Manager's SRE & Operations Playbook

Six playbooks, the frameworks behind them, copy-paste templates, and technical depth for the senior manager who runs SRE and operations for an AI product. It covers incidents, and also the daily work of running the team, the operation, and the politics: leading a contract NOC, SREs across the US and India, and an Ops lead who's still growing, while managing up to a demanding, highly technical director. Every page uses Nimbus, a hybrid on-device and cloud AI assistant, as its worked example. The practices apply anywhere.

In an Incident

  1. Is It an Incident?If your NOC is unsure, use the Triage or Incident? checker. When in doubt, declare.
  2. Find the ScenarioUse the triage tool below to see what you should ask and decide, and what your NOC and SREs should be doing.
  3. Ask the Right QuestionsWork through LAYERS: Locate, Assess, Yesterday, Evidence, Response, Sustain. Keep the pocket card open.
  4. Update UpwardBuild an executive update with the SITREP builder. Bottom line first.
  5. AfterwardsChair the post-incident review and follow the actions through.

Incident Triage

Live

This Week

A weekly rhythm that keeps the operation healthy, the team growing, and your director informed. Tick items off as you go. The list resets in your browser only.

  • Daily: run the SRE daily in the overlap hours. Walk the board right to left, and PULSE what's stuck.
  • Before the daily: check your Linear views for aging, blocked, and not-ready work.
  • Cycle start: run cycle planning with the capacity split, and update the RAID log.
  • Monday: send the weekly reliability note (or review your Ops lead's draft).
  • Vendor sync with the NOC delivery manager: scorecard trends, staffing, one improvement (how).
  • 1:1s with SREs in both regions and your Ops lead. Their agenda first (template).
  • Postmortem actions: review overdue items and unblock one with its owner's leader.
  • Follow-the-sun: read the handoffs. Is one region carrying the pain? (fairness)
  • Managing up: send your director a technical pre-read before any review.
  • Stakeholders: one deposit with a CFT or Cloud Infrastructure leader (political capital).
  • Learn: one Technical Corner module, ideally with your Ops lead.
  • Recognize: thank one person by name, in public, for specific work (morale).

How This Playbook Thinks

Ask, Don't Grab the Keyboard

Your leverage is the right question at the right layer, not doing the investigation yourself.

Mitigate, Then Debug

Stop the bleeding first. Root cause can wait until users are unblocked.

Absorb Pressure, Pass On Clarity

Shield the team from noise without hiding information they need.

No Surprises

Pre-wire decisions, update on a cadence, and tell bad news early.

Learn Every Time

Blameless postmortems, owned actions, and follow-through you can show.

Grow Everyone

Contractors, SREs in both regions, and your Ops lead all get a path.
