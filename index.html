How This Guide Thinks

Coordinate and Investigate Separately

One person runs the room, and another runs the problem.

Mitigate, Then Debug

Stop the bleeding first. Root cause can wait until users are unblocked.

Layer First

Device, edge, gateway, orchestrator, service, or model? The answer tells you the owner.

Learn Every Time

Every incident leaves a runbook, and serious ones get a blameless postmortem.
