How to Use This Playbook

This is your playbook as a senior manager of reliability and service operations. Use it at different moments: in an incident, in your weekly rhythm, before a hard conversation, or when you want to get sharper technically. Find your moment below.

During a Live Incident

  1. Press In an Incident (top right) to open the triage tool. It shows your questions and decisions first, then what your NOC and SREs should be doing.
  2. Ask questions with LAYERS, and decide using Decisions Only You Can Make.
  3. Update executives with the SITREP builder. Shield the responders from side questions.

Tip: click the # next to any heading to copy a link to that section, then paste it into Slack or Teams.

In Your Weekly Rhythm

Work through the This Week checklist on the home page. Report with Metrics & Reporting, and manage the vendor with Managing a Contract NOC.

Leading People

Before 1:1s, feedback, or career conversations, open 1:1s, Feedback & Performance. For your Ops lead, use the 30/60/90 plan. For SREs across regions, see Leading Global SREs. Onboard new people with Hiring & Onboarding.

Managing Up and Across

Before an important meeting, map stakeholders and pre-wire (Stakeholders & Influence). For a difficult director, read Managing Up. When you need a decision, write a Decision Memo.

Learning the Technical Side

Follow the Technical Corner learning path, one module a week. Use the Failure Catalog as flashcards and KQL Training to check claims yourself.

Practice and Post-Incident Learning

Run a tabletop exercise each month with your team (Morgan, the director archetype, shows up in the injects). Chair postmortems and track action items. Score responses with Weak / Good / Best.

Copy and Paste

Every template lives in the Template Library, with a filter and copy buttons. Print the LAYERS pocket card for your desk.

Reusing It Elsewhere

Start with Using This Guide Anywhere, then the generic KQL toolkit and postmortem template.
