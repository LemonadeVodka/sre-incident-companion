How to Use This Guide

This guide is used at different moments by different people. Find your moment below and follow its path.

During a Live Incident

Who: the NOC Coordinator, the SRE Technical Lead, and managers joining.

  1. Press In an Incident (top right) to open the triage tool.
  2. NOC: Triage & Escalate, then Coordinate. SRE: Investigate. Managers: During an Incident.
  3. Open the matching playbook.

Tip: click the # next to any heading to copy a link to that section, then paste it into Slack or Teams so everyone sees the same steps.

Onboarding

Who: new NOC engineers, SREs, and managers.

Follow your role's onboarding path (week 1, month 1, month 3). Learn to query with KQL Training. Your progress is saved in your browser. Print your role card.

Tabletop Exercises and Game Days

Who: a facilitator plus one person per role. Allow 45–60 minutes.

Open the Tabletop Kit, pick a scenario, assign roles, and reveal injects on the timer. Expect Morgan the director to show up. Score the debrief with Weak / Good / Best and export the summary.

After an Incident

Who: the NOC (timeline), SRE (author), CFTs, and the senior manager (chair).

Follow the postmortem process: write it with the writer, run the review, and track the action items. Update the playbook you used.

Leadership and Monthly Reviews

Who: senior managers and directors.

Use the monthly health check, the maturity scorecard, and What Good Looks Like. When asking for budget or tooling, link the tooling asks.

Reusing It Elsewhere

Who: other teams and organizations.

Start with Using This Guide Anywhere, then the generic KQL toolkit and postmortem template.
