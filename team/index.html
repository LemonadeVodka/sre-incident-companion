People & Team Playbook

You lead three very different groups: a contract NOC you manage through a vendor, FTE SREs split across the US and India, and an Ops lead who's growing into the technical side. Each needs a different approach, and all of them need protecting from a demanding environment. This playbook covers how.

  • Contract NOC: set outcomes through the vendor. Don't manage contractors like employees.
  • SREs: one team in two regions. Make on-call, interesting work, and visibility fair.
  • Ops lead: a 30/60/90 plan, with you as coach and the TPM work as practice.
  • Everyone: regular 1:1s, specific feedback, and structural fixes for burnout.

Regular 1:1s, fair on-call, clear growth plans, and the vendor held to outcomes.

People grow into bigger roles in both regions, and the team stays steady under pressure.
