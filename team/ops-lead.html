Developing Your Ops Lead

Your Operations lead keeps the machine running: rotations, the vendor relationship day to day, reporting, follow-ups. They're organized and committed, and they're working on the technical side. Developing them is one of your highest-leverage investments. A technically confident Ops lead takes a whole layer of work off your plate and earns credibility with SREs and your director.

  • Assess skill and will for each responsibility, and lead differently for each.
  • Give them a 30/60/90 technical plan built on the Technical Corner and KQL Training, with real deliverables.
  • Pair them with an SRE weekly, and have them shadow incidents as a learner, not a coordinator.
  • Make their progress visible, especially to a technical director.

Skill / Will Assessment

For each part of the role, decide where they are, and lead accordingly (situational leadership):

High willLow will
High skillDelegate. Give ownership and stay out of the way. Example: vendor governance.Excite. Find the meaning, connect it to their goals.
Low skillGuide. Teach, pair, and check in often. Example: reading latency and error charts.Direct. Give clear instructions and short loops, and find out what's behind the low will.

30 / 60 / 90 Technical Plan

First 30 Days: Understand

  • Complete Know the Layers and the Latency & Errors Primer. Explain the layers back to you from memory.
  • Complete KQL Module 1, and run the shift sweep themselves three times.
  • Shadow two incidents as an observer, and write down which layer it was and how the team knew.

Days 31–60: Apply

  • Complete KQL Module 2, and score 9/12 or better on the quiz.
  • Build the weekly latency and error summary by layer yourself, and present it in the weekly review.
  • Pair weekly with an SRE. Ask them to explain one investigation using LAYERS.
  • Facilitate the SRE daily once a week as your TPM apprentice, then debrief with your manager on which PULSE questions moved work.

Days 61–90: Lead

  • Run the NOC vendor's monthly scorecard review on your own.
  • Draft the executive SITREP for a Sev 2 (you review it before it goes out).
  • Lead a tabletop as facilitator, using the kit.
  • Present one technical topic to the team, for example "how 429s happen on shared model capacity".

Pairing and Shadowing

  • Weekly 30-minute SRE pairing. Rotate SREs across both regions, so they also build relationships.
  • In incidents: the Ops lead watches the Technical Lead's workstream and writes notes. Afterwards, the SRE spends 10 minutes on "what were you looking at and why?"
  • Learning log. Each week, one thing learned and one question. Review it in your 1:1.

Making Progress Visible

A highly technical director may underrate a less technical Ops lead. Change that perception with evidence, not argument:

  • Have the Ops lead present the weekly latency and error summary in a leadership review, with your support.
  • Credit them by name for operational wins in your SITREPs and reports.
  • Let them own a visible improvement, such as a vendor KPI moving the right way.

Use the 30/60/90 template to track it.
