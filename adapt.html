Using This Guide Anywhere

Nimbus is a worked example. The roles, the process, the templates, and the Weak / Good / Best levels apply to any team running a production service. This page shows what to keep, what to swap, and how to map our terms to yours.

  • Keep: the separation of coordinator and technical lead, mitigate before you debug, timed escalation, blameless postmortems, and Weak / Good / Best.
  • Swap: team names, tools, the architecture layers, KQL parameter values, and the baselines.
  • Start small: pick one role track and one playbook, and adapt those first.

Keep As-Is

Roles

Coordinator, technical lead, owners, and a senior manager who decides and unblocks. These work at any size.

Process

Detect, triage, declare, mitigate, resolve, learn. Severity follows user impact.

Templates

Updates, action requests, handovers, and the postmortem. They're written generically.

Weak / Good / Best

Use it as a self-assessment, a tabletop rubric, and a postmortem lens.

Replace

  • The architecture page: draw your own request path, owners, and blind spots, the same way as Background: Nimbus.
  • Baselines: your own P50/P95 and error rates per feature.
  • KQL values: set them once in Make It Yours, or use the generic KQL toolkit.
  • Escalation ladder: your teams, your paging tool, your timers.
  • Pre-agreed requests: the low-risk actions your owners will take on request.
  • Playbooks: keep the shape (symptoms, checks, causes, verify, roles, Weak/Good/Best, postmortem focus) and swap the specifics.
