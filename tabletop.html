Playbook 1 · Incident Management

Tabletop Exercise Kit

The cheapest way to get better at incidents is to practice them. Gather a coordinator, a technical lead, a manager, and a facilitator. Pick a scenario, reveal what happens next on a timer, and score how it went.

  • 45–60 minutes: 5 minutes of setup, 25–30 of play, and 15 of debrief.
  • The facilitator reads the injects and plays the CFTs, Cloud Infrastructure, and Morgan the director.
  • The debrief is scored with Weak / Good / Best. Copy the summary into the team channel or a ticket.
Morgan appears in every scenario. The deep-diving director DMs responders, asks for raw queries, starts side calls, and demands root cause mid-mitigation. Handling Morgan is part of the exercise, and it's scored as Managing Stakeholder Pressure.

Facilitator Tips

Stay in Character

Play the slow CFT and the defensive platform team realistically. That's where the learning is.

Don't Rescue

Let the group struggle a little. Hints come only when they're truly stuck.

Make Morgan Real

Be persistent but well-meaning. See whether the coordinator names a comms buffer.

Blameless Debrief

Score the system and the process, not the people. Turn every Weak into one action item.
