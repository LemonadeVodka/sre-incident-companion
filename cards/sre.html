SRE Role Card

Joining

  • Acknowledge, join #inc-, read the pinned record
  • "I'm Technical Lead. @NOC is IC."
  • Re-run the impact query yourself
  • Tell the IC your first hypothesis

Find the Layer

  • Device: routing split, fallback rate
  • Edge: Front Door 5xx / 403
  • Gateway: APIM 401, latency
  • Cloud orchestrator: everything slow together
  • Feature service: by instance, panics
  • Model: 429, 5xx, first token

Recommend a Mitigation

  • Action · owner · evidence · risk · undo
  • Prefer pause, halt, shift, or roll back
  • One change at a time
  • Costs money or quality? Manager decides via the IC

Rust Signatures

  • Runtime stall: one instance slow, CPU moderate
  • Pool exhaustion: gap before dependency spans
  • Panic: restarts, APIM 502s, "panicked at"

Protect Your Focus

  • Route DMs (yes, Morgan's) to the IC
  • Share one chart, not raw KQL
  • "@IC can you cover questions for 15 min?"

After

  • Confirm 30 min stable
  • Save queries as evidence
  • Technical analysis draft in 2 days
