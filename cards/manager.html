Senior Manager · The LAYERS Framework

LAYERS Pocket Card

L · Locate

  • NOC: Which feature? One version, region, or partner, or everyone?
  • SRE: Which layer, and how do you know?
  • Exec: "It's in [layer], affecting [features]."

A · Assess

  • NOC: Number now vs normal? Trend?
  • SRE: P50 or only P95? Counting user-visible failures?
  • Exec: "[X%] of [feature] users, for [time]."

Y · Yesterday

  • NOC: Same time yesterday? Any release or partner notice?
  • SRE: What changed in the hour before it started?
  • Exec: "Started at [time], lines up with [change]."

E · Evidence

  • NOC: Which query shows it? Paste the link.
  • SRE: Confidence? What would prove us wrong?
  • Exec: "We're [confident / still confirming]."

R · Response

  • NOC: Owner paged? Which pre-agreed request?
  • SRE: Safest reversible fix? What do you need from me?
  • Exec: "We're [action]. Decision needed: [X]."

S · Sustain

  • NOC: What would have helped you see it earlier?
  • SRE: Detection, design, or process gap? One high-leverage fix?
  • Exec: "To prevent recurrence: [actions, dates]."

Only You Decide

  • Spend · quality trade-offs · which feature to protect
  • Disable a partner · failover · halt a release
  • Customer messaging · pulling in people

Stay Out of the Weeds

  • Ask what you need to decide, not how it works
  • One channel, no DMs, no side calls
  • Be the comms buffer for other leaders
