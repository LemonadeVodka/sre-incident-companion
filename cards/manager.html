Senior Manager · Decide, Unblock, Follow Through

Manager Role Card

Ask Once

  • Who's coordinating (NOC IC)?
  • What's the impact, in numbers?
  • What's the mitigation, and when's the next update?
  • What do you need from me?

Decisions Only You Make

  • Spend (spillover, capacity)
  • Quality vs availability
  • Which feature to protect
  • Failover (residency, cost)
  • Halting releases
  • Customer and public messaging

Unblock

  • CFT silent 45 min: call their leadership
  • Cloud Infrastructure disputes: escalate peer to peer with data
  • Stalled NOC requests: you can unblock them

Don't Be Morgan

  • No root-cause questions mid-incident
  • No DMs to responders
  • No side calls
  • Be the comms buffer instead

After

  • Thank responders by name
  • Chair the Sev 1–2 postmortem
  • Fund action items, review monthly
