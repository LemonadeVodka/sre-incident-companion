NOC · Incident Coordinator

NOC Role Card

Triage or Incident?

  • Watch: odd, normal range, no users. Log it
  • Triage: maybe users. Thread + 15-min time box
  • Incident: users + above normal 10 min, OR shared layer, OR need help
  • Unsure after 15 min? Declare

First 15 Minutes

  • Acknowledge in PagerDuty (≤ 5 min)
  • Confirm with the sweep query, compared with yesterday
  • Scope: feature · layer · client version · region
  • Propose a severity (if unsure, go higher)
  • Declare and open #inc-YYYYMMDD-name, then pin the record
  • Page the owner and SRE (Technical Lead) through PagerDuty
  • Bridge to Teams-only CFTs (Collab, Device)

Every 10–15 Minutes (IC Loop)

  • Impact now?
  • Workstreams and owners?
  • Who's silent? Check the timers
  • Decision waiting? Get it made
  • Update: Slack + Teams mirror + record

Escalation Timers

  • 15 min: re-page, ask in their channel
  • 30 min: page the CFT manager, tell the senior manager
  • 45 min: senior manager contacts CFT leadership
  • Sev 1: halve all timers

Requests, Not Actions

  • @owner ACTION REQUEST: [action] · why · check back at HH:MM
  • Pre-agreed: pause batch · halt rollout · restart one instance · scale out · banner
  • Everything else goes through the Technical Lead

Executive Interruptions

  • "Thanks. Next update at HH:MM, and it'll answer that."
  • Name a comms buffer at declaration
  • Keep decisions in the Slack channel

Closing

  • 30 min stable, then resolve and send the final update
  • Jira ticket within 24 h
  • Schedule the postmortem before standing down
