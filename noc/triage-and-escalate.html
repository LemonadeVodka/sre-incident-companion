Triage & Escalate

From the first alert to a declared incident with the right people working on it, in 15 minutes. You don't need a runbook for this. The same steps work for every incident. You'll stay on as Incident Coordinator afterwards.

  • Confirm the problem, identify the layer, propose a severity, and page through PagerDuty.
  • The layer tells you who to page: edge or auth points to Cloud Infrastructure, one feature points to its CFT, and anything shared or unclear goes to SRE.
  • Open the Slack channel and pinned record. Bridge to Teams-only CFTs.
  • Start the escalation timer the moment you page. Brief the Technical Lead (SRE) when they join. You stay IC.

The First 15 Minutes

  • Min 0–2: Acknowledge in PagerDuty.
  • Min 2–5: Confirm. Run the matching sweep query and compare with yesterday. Is it real and sustained?
  • Min 5–8: Scope. Which feature(s), which layer (table below), which client version, which region?
  • Min 8–10: Propose a severity using the severity matrix. If unsure, go higher.
  • Min 10–12: Declare and open #inc-YYYYMMDD-name in Slack and pin the incident record.
  • Min 12–15: Page the owner and SRE on-call through PagerDuty, with the escalation message below. Start the timer.
  • If a Teams-only CFT is involved, open the Teams chat and post the mirror message.

Paging in PagerDuty

  1. Create an incident on the owner's PagerDuty service. If the CFT has no service, use the "Nimbus CFT leads" escalation policy.
  2. Set the urgency.High for Sev 1–2, low for Sev 3.
  3. Add SRE on-call as an additional responder (the "Nimbus SRE" service) for Sev 1–2, shared layers, or anything unclear.
  4. Paste the escalation message (below) as the incident note, including the Slack channel link.
  5. Write the page time in the pinned record. The timer starts now.
TextEscalation messagePagerDuty note + first post in the incident channel
[Sev X proposed] Nimbus [feature or layer] — [symptom]
Impact: [e.g. Device Care errors 9% (baseline <1%) since 14:05 UTC]
Scope: [features / layer / client versions / region]
Checked: [sweep queries 1, 5 — links]; Azure status [green/red]; recent changes [none/…]
Requested: [nothing yet / pause Proactive Tips batch from Insights CFT at 14:18]
Channel: Slack #inc-… · Teams bridge: [name or n/a]
Need: [owner to investigate X / SRE as Technical Lead]

Bridging to Teams

Two CFTs, Collab (Meeting Recap) and Device (Device Care), work in Microsoft Teams and can't see our Slack workflows. As Incident Coordinator, you are the bridge unless you assign a second NOC person:

  • Open a Teams chat with the same name as the Slack channel, and add the CFT on-call or lead.
  • Post the mirror message at every update, and relay their replies to Slack prefixed with [from Teams].
  • If they want to talk it through, start a Teams meeting and invite the IC.

The full ladder, including the manager rungs, is on Ways of working. Halve the timers for Sev 1.

Briefing the Technical Lead

When SRE joins, you stay Incident Coordinator. Give them the technical picture in one message, then keep running the incident.
