Common Mistakes

No Time Filter

Slow, and it mixes today with last week. Filter on time first.

Count() on Sampled Data

Undercounts. Use sum(itemCount) on Application Insights tables.

Whole-Number Division

failed / total becomes 0. Write 100.0 * failed / total.

Averages for Latency

They hide the slow tail. Use P50 and P95.

Wrong Table Spelling

requests/timestamp (classic) vs AppRequests/TimeGenerated (workspace).

No Baseline

A number alone means nothing. Compare with yesterday or last week.
