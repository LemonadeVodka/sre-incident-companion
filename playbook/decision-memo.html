Decision Memo

The fastest way to get a decision from busy or skeptical leaders is a one-page memo that does the thinking for them: why now, the options, what you recommend, what it costs, and exactly what you're asking for. It's especially effective with a highly technical director, because it shows rigor up front.

  • One page. If it needs more, put it in an appendix.
  • Always include at least two real options plus "do nothing", with its cost.
  • Lead with the ask and the deadline.
  • Pre-wire it before the meeting (how).

Structure

  1. The AskThe decision you need, from whom, and by when.
  2. ContextThe problem in three sentences, with data: user impact, incidents, cost.
  3. OptionsTwo or three, plus "do nothing". For each: what it is, cost, time, risk, benefit.
  4. RecommendationWhich option and why, in two sentences.
  5. Risks and MitigationsWhat could go wrong, and what you'll do about it.
  6. Who's AlignedWho you've pre-wired, and what they said.

Example

ExampleSeparate Capacity for Background AI Work
ASK: Approve a dedicated model deployment for background workloads (Next Step batch)
     by Oct 20, about $X/month. Decision: Director of Reliability + Cloud Infrastructure lead.

CONTEXT: Background and customer-facing work share one model deployment. In the last
quarter, 3 incidents (2 Sev 2) were caused by background bursts throttling Device Care.
About 38,000 users affected in total. Each incident cost ~6 engineer-hours plus credibility.

OPTIONS:
 A. Dedicated background deployment. ~$X/mo. 2 weeks. Removes the cause.
 B. Rate-limit background work at the gateway. ~$0. 1 week. Reduces risk; tips arrive later.
 C. Do nothing. $0. Expect ~1 Sev 2 per month at current growth.

RECOMMENDATION: B now (fast, free), A next quarter as volume grows.

RISKS: B delays tips by up to 30 min at peak (acceptable per Insights CFT).

ALIGNED: Insights CFT EM (agrees), Cloud Infrastructure lead (prefers A, accepts B first),
Device CFT EM (strongly supports).
