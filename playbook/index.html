Framework Index

A small set of frameworks, used consistently, makes you predictable to your team, credible to your director, and fast in a crisis. Each one lives in the playbook where you use it. This page is the map: what each one is for, and where to find it.

Incident Management

FrameworkUse it whenIn one line
Triage or Incident?Something looks off and nobody's sureWatch → Triage (15 min) → Incident. When in doubt, declare.
LAYERSAny latency or error problem, live or in reviewLocate, Assess, Yesterday, Evidence, Response, Sustain, asked at the right layer
SeverityDeciding how big an incident isSeverity follows user impact. If unsure, go higher.
SITREP & BLUFUpdating executivesBottom line first, then impact, cause, actions, decisions, next update

Post-Incident & Problem Management

FrameworkUse it whenIn one line
After Action ReviewAfter any significant incidentWhat was supposed to happen, what happened, why the difference, what to sustain and improve
Postmortem Quality RubricReviewing a postmortem draftBlameless, evidence-based, actions that change the system

Execution & Delivery (TPM Hat)

FrameworkUse it whenIn one line
