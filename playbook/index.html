All Frameworks

A small set of frameworks, used consistently, makes you predictable to your team, credible to your director, and fast in a crisis. Each one below tells you when to use it and links to the full page and its copy-paste template.

Incidents and Operations

FrameworkUse it whenIn one line
Triage or Incident?Something looks off and nobody's sureWatch → Triage (15 min) → Incident. When in doubt, declare.
LAYERSAny latency or error problem, live or in reviewLocate, Assess, Yesterday, Evidence, Response, Sustain, asked at the right layer
SeverityDeciding how big an incident isSeverity follows user impact. If unsure, go higher.
SITREP & BLUFUpdating executivesBottom line first, then situation, impact, cause, actions, decisions, next update
After Action ReviewAfter any significant incidentSupposed to happen, what happened, why the difference, sustain and improve
Weak / Good / BestAssessing a response or a teamNine dimensions, three levels
