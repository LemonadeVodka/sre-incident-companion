RACI & Severity

Two frameworks that prevent the most common arguments in incidents: "whose job is this?" and "how big is this?". Agree them before incidents, write them down, and point to them when tension rises.

RACI

Responsible (does the work), Accountable (one person who owns the outcome), Consulted (input before), Informed (told after).

If two people are Accountable, nobody is. Resolve disputes by agreeing the A before the next incident, and pre-wire it with both leaders.

Severity

Severity follows user impact. The canonical definitions, with Nimbus examples and the response for each level, are on Incident Process. Here's the short version:

SeverityIn one line
Sev 1Most users or several features badly affected. Page now, SITREP every 30 min.
Sev 2One important feature badly affected, or partial impact across features. SITREP every 60 min.
Sev 3Minor, contained, or a workaround exists. Business hours.
Sev 4No user impact yet, but a risk. Backlog.

If unsure, go higher. Downgrading costs one message. See Triage or Incident? for the step before severity.
