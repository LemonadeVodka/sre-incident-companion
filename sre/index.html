SRE on-call

The NOC got the right people moving. Now you take command, find which layer is failing, drive mitigation, and pull in CFTs and Platform, many of whom are slow to respond or on another chat platform. Because we're a new team, there often won't be a runbook. You'll be writing it as you go.

  • You are the default Incident Commander for Sev 1–2, unless you hand IC to someone else explicitly.
  • Start with the layer (edge, gateway, service, model). It tells you the owner and narrows the causes.
  • Mitigate before you debug. Chase non-responding CFTs on a timer, not on hope.
  • Every incident ends with a Jira ticket, a runbook owner, and a postmortem date.

Taking the handoff from the NOC

  • Acknowledge in PagerDuty and join the #inc- channel.
  • Read the pinned record and the NOC handoff. Ask about anything unclear once, in the channel.
  • Say "I have IC" in the channel, or name someone else as IC.
  • Confirm or change the severity, and say why.
  • Assign roles: bridge (Teams CFTs), scribe, and ops lead if it isn't you.
  • Check the NOC's actions: are they still in effect, and did they help?
  • Set the next update time, and stick to it.

Your first 10 minutes after handoff

  1. Confirm impact yourself.Re-run the NOC's query. Numbers drift, so confirm they still hold.
  2. Find the layer.Run the layer comparison query. Edge, gateway, service, or model?
  3. Check for a change.Deploys, client rollout, config or policy changes in AzureActivity, prompt or model version changes.
  4. Pick a mitigation if one is obvious.Roll back, pause, shift, or degrade. Announce it, then do it.
  5. Make sure the owner is engaged.If not, climb the ladder.

Working with the NOC · they're your eyes and your bridge

Do

  • Thank them for good handoffs, and tell them what was missing when a handoff was weak.
  • Keep them as bridge or scribe, because they already have context.
  • Tell them when a pre-approved action can be undone.

Grow them

  • Explain your reasoning out loud in the channel.
  • Invite NOC engineers to shadow your on-call shifts.
  • Turn their noisy-alert notes into runbook work.
