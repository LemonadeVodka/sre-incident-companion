Incident command

Being IC in a mature organization is hard enough. Here you also bridge two chat platforms, chase CFTs without on-call rotations, and keep records outside Jira. This page is the IC's operating manual for that reality.

  • The IC coordinates and decides. Delegate the debugging as soon as there's anyone to delegate to.
  • Assign bridge and scribe early, and keep the pinned record current.
  • Chase CFTs on the ladder timers, and bring in the senior manager for decisions and stuck escalations.
  • Close with a Jira ticket, a runbook owner, and a postmortem date, every time.

The IC loop

Repeat this loop every 10 to 15 minutes until the incident is resolved:

  1. What's the impact now?Re-check the user-facing number.
  2. What are we trying, and who's on it?Each workstream has one name next to it.
  3. Who haven't we heard from?Check the ladder timers for every paged team.
  4. Is any decision waiting?Make it, or escalate it to the senior manager.
  5. Update.Slack update, Teams mirror (through the bridge), and pinned record.

Running across Slack and Teams

  • Decisions only count in Slack. If something is agreed in a Teams call, the bridge posts it in Slack with [from Teams], and only then does it count.
  • Join the Teams call yourself if a Teams-only CFT is the main investigator. The bridge keeps Slack in sync.
  • Use the Slack workflows (declare, timeline). They don't exist in Teams, so the Slack channel remains the record.
  • Update templates are on Incident process. The Teams mirror message is on Ways of working.

Keep it factual

Record page and acknowledgement times in the pinned record without commentary, for example "Collab CFT paged 14:12, acked 14:51". Those numbers are what leadership uses to fix CFT on-call coverage.

Bringing in the senior manager

Bring them in for decisions and unblocking, not status. Be specific about what you need:

TextDecision requestPost in the channel and @ the senior manager
@[manager] decision needed by HH:MM:
Option A: [e.g. enable paygo spillover] — fixes [X], costs ~[$/h]
Option B: [e.g. pause Proactive Tips for the day] — fixes [X], impact [Y]
Recommendation: [A/B] because [..]

Typical decisions: extra spend, quality-for-availability trade-offs, failover with data-residency implications, customer or partner messaging, and pulling in people from other teams. See what managers decide.

Closing the incident

  • Impact at baseline for 30 minutes. Post the resolution update in Slack, mirror it to Teams, and notify PagerDuty.
  • Temporary actions still in effect (paused batch, halted rollout, scale-out) have an owner and an undo time.
  • File the Jira ticket within 24 hours, using the pinned record (how).
  • Assign a runbook owner and a due date (3 business days). New team: a rough runbook beats none.
  • Schedule the postmortem (required for Sev 1–2) and invite every CFT involved, Teams users included.
  • Note the CFT response times and any tooling gaps for the monthly review.
