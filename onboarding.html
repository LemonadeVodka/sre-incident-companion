Onboarding Paths

A structured path for each role, from first-week reading to running real incidents. Tick items off as you go. Progress is saved in this browser only.

  • Each path mixes reading, practice (tabletops, shadowing), and doing.
  • Pair every new person with a buddy from the same role.
  • Managers: check in at the end of week 1, month 1, and month 3.

NOC: Incident Coordinator

Week 1

  • Read Background: Nimbus. Draw the request path from memory, including both orchestrators.
  • Read the NOC role and the Good column for your role on What Good Looks Like.
  • Run the shift health sweep alongside your buddy on three shifts.
  • Check that PagerDuty, Slack, and Teams access all work. Join the Collab and Device CFT Teams channels.
  • Print the NOC role card.

Month 1

  • Read Triage & Escalate, Coordinate, and Requesting Actions.
  • Play the Coordinator in a tabletop exercise that includes Morgan.
  • Shadow a real incident as scribe or bridge.
  • Write up one confusing alert as a runbook draft.

Month 3

  • Coordinate a Sev 3 as IC, with your buddy as backup.
  • Hand over a postmortem-ready timeline, and attend the review.
  • Coordinate a Sev 2 tabletop scoring "Good" or better on Coordination and Stakeholder Pressure.

SRE: Technical Lead

Week 1

  • Read Background, SRE role, and Ways of Working.
  • Run every query on Nimbus KQL once, and note which ones return nothing (telemetry gaps).
  • Meet your CFT liaison counterparts, and read their service cards.

Month 1

  • Read Investigate and Leading the Technical Response.
  • Shadow two incidents as a second investigator.
  • Play Technical Lead in a tabletop, including the fallback-surge scenario.
  • Write the technical analysis for one postmortem.

Month 3

  • Primary on-call with a backup. Be Technical Lead for a Sev 2.
  • Read Deep Dives, and run a game day with one CFT.
  • Close one postmortem action item that changes the system.

Senior Manager

Week 1

  • Read the manager path, During an Incident, and the jargon decoder.
  • Read When a Leader Dives Too Deep.
  • Print the manager role card.

Month 1

  • Join a tabletop as the manager, and practice being the comms buffer for Morgan.
  • Chair one postmortem review using the agenda.
  • Meet the Cloud Infrastructure and CFT leads about response expectations.

Month 3

  • Run the first monthly action-item review.
  • Pick one tooling ask (for example, NOC tooling) and get it funded.
  • Score the team on What Good Looks Like, and set one dimension to improve this quarter.
