Hiring & Onboarding

Plans you run as the manager: your own first 90 days, plus onboarding for contract NOC staff, new SREs, and your Ops lead. Tick items off as you go. Progress is saved in this browser only.

  • Each plan mixes reading, practice (tabletops, shadowing), and doing.
  • Pair every new person with a buddy. For SREs, pick a buddy in the other region too.
  • Contract NOC onboarding runs through the vendor: you set the outcomes, and they manage their people.

Hiring

  • Agree the bar before interviews with the SRE hiring scorecard.
  • Use the same interview loop in the US and India, so the bar is identical in both regions.
  • Include a troubleshooting exercise based on a real-shaped failure.
  • For the NOC vendor, evaluate with the vendor evaluation, not individual interviews (why).

Your First 90 Days

Days 1–30: Listen and Learn

  • Read Background: Nimbus and Know the Layers.
  • 1:1 with every SRE (both regions), your Ops lead, the NOC delivery manager, and each CFT and Cloud Infrastructure lead.
  • Map your stakeholders (template), including your director.
  • Read the last five postmortems. Count open actions.

Days 31–60: Set the Rhythm

  • Start the weekly reliability note and the vendor scorecard.
  • Chair a postmortem review using the agenda.
  • Run a tabletop as facilitator, including Morgan's injects.
  • Agree RACI and severity with CFT and Cloud Infrastructure leads (framework).

Days 61–90: Make Your First Bets

  • Score the team on Weak / Good / Best, and pick one dimension to improve.
  • Get one investment funded with a decision memo (for example, NOC tooling).
  • Run the first monthly action-item review.

New Contract NOC Staff

Share this plan with the vendor's delivery manager as the expected outcome. The vendor schedules and coaches its people.

  • Week 1: Background: Nimbus, the request path, and access to PagerDuty, Slack, and Teams.
  • Week 1: KQL Module 1, and the shift health sweep alongside a buddy for three shifts.
  • Month 1: Triage or Incident?, Incident Operations, and KQL Module 2 with 9/12 or better on the quiz.
  • Month 1: play Coordinator in a tabletop.
  • Month 3: coordinate a Sev 3 as IC with backup, and hand over a postmortem-ready timeline.

New SREs (US and India)

  • Week 1: Background, Ways of Working, and how the global team works. Meet your buddy in the other region.
  • Week 1: all three KQL modules. Run every query on Nimbus KQL once and note the telemetry gaps.
  • Month 1: Troubleshooting Walkthrough and Incident Operations. Shadow two incidents.
  • Month 1: play Technical Lead in a tabletop, and write the technical analysis for one postmortem.
  • Month 3: primary on-call with backup. Read Deep Dives and close one action item that changes the system.
