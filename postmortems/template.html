Postmortem Template & Writer

Fill in the form and the postmortem writes itself as Markdown, ready to paste into a Jira ticket, a wiki, or a doc. The draft is saved in this browser only, so you can close the tab and come back.

  • Write for a reader who wasn't there, such as a senior manager or a new hire.
  • Use blameless language: "the deploy was approved because the check didn't cover X", not "Sam broke it".
  • Contributing factors come in the plural. Incidents rarely have one cause.
  • Action items: owner | action | due date | type, one per line.

Blameless Language

Instead ofWrite
"Alex pushed a bad config.""A config change reached production. The review step didn't check X, and there was no canary."
"The CFT ignored the page.""The page went to a channel the CFT doesn't watch, and they had no PagerDuty service."
"Cloud Infrastructure caused it.""A change to the shared gateway wasn't announced to the teams that depend on it."
"The director slowed us down.""With no exec update format and no comms buffer, leadership questions went straight to responders."
"Root cause: human error.""Contributing factors: [the conditions that made the mistake easy and its impact large]."
